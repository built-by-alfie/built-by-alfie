The nutrition approach

NUTRITION
BY NUMBERS.

No generic meal plans. Your calorie and nutrition targets are set from your bodyweight, height, age, activity and training demands.

01
Energy balance
Calorie intake is structured around energy expenditure. For fat loss, the objective is a sustainable energy deficit — grounded in the basic thermodynamics of energy balance.
02
Macro targets
Protein, carbohydrates and fats are allocated to support the goal, training performance, recovery and adherence.
03
Fibre & micronutrients
Food choices are structured to support fibre intake and broad micronutrient coverage rather than relying on restrictive food lists.
04
Adjust as you progress
Targets can be refined using changes in bodyweight, performance, recovery and adherence rather than forcing a fixed plan indefinitely.
